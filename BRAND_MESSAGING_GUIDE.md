- **Primary Tagline:** **Explore More. Discover More.**
- **Brand Positioning Line:** **See Beyond the Profile**
- **Mandatory Disclosure:** Viewora is an independent analytics platform and is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta**.

---

## 1. Core Brand Voice & Integrity Rules

1. **Transparent & Evidence-First:** Always distinguish observed public/authorized metrics from unavailable fields (`Unavailable`) and deterministic templates from live AI outputs.
2. **Compliance as a Competitive Advantage:** Never imply private Instagram access, password-based tracking, "secret viewer" capabilities, or unauthorized scraping.
3. **Calm Enterprise Polish:** Clear typography, restrained violet/indigo accents, high contrast, zero hype jargon, and zero fabricated social proof.
